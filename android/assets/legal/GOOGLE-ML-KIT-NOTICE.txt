Terms and privacy:
https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/terms

Data disclosure guidance:
https://developers.google.com/ml-kit/android-data-disclosure

Attribution requirements and official badge source:
https://docs.cloud.google.com/translate/attribution
https://docs.cloud.google.com/static/translate/images/google-translate-attribution.zip

Translation input and output are processed on device and are not sent to Google
servers by ML Kit. The SDK may contact Google to obtain models, fixes, and
compatibility information. Google states that ML Kit also sends performance and
utilization metrics, including device/app information, diagnostics, event/error
information, and configured translation languages.
